## Short Version

The current best synthetic workflow can recover the known 3-rebar
variable-depth/variable-radius geometry under noisy, source-mismatched,
ringdown-stressed conditions. Across output experiments 740-1218, the
structured report parsed 425 coordinate-optimizer runs. All 425 selected the
exact known x/z/r geometry.

The remaining problem is not usually localization. It is confidence separation:
159 of the 425 exact-geometry rows remained below the strict base-margin cutoff
of 5.0e-4. In plain terms, the optimizer often picks the right rebar geometry,
but the best radius is sometimes only slightly better than the next competing
radius.

## Terms Clarified

- Reference data, in the synthetic experiments, means the synthetic observed
  radar waveform generated by running FDTD on the known true rebar scene. It is
  not real field data.
- FDTD means finite-difference time-domain simulation. It is the forward radar
  simulator used to produce waveforms from a candidate concrete/rebar scene.
- Objective means the scoring rule used to compare a candidate waveform against
  the reference waveform. It is a waveform mismatch, also called a misfit. Lower
  misfit is better.
- Base objective means the default scoring rule used for the main acceptance
  decision.
- Objective variants are diagnostic scoring rules using different time windows
  or frequency bands. They check whether the same answer remains strong under
  alternate views of the waveform.
- Target0, target1, and target2 are the three synthetic rebars:
  - target0: shallow-left rebar, x=150 mm, z=80 mm, radius=5 mm
  - target1: center rebar, x=250 mm, z=100 mm, radius=6 mm
  - target2: deeper-right rebar, x=350 mm, z=120 mm, radius=8 mm
- Seed means the repeatable synthetic noise/source-mismatch draw. It is not a
  different physical rebar layout.

## What Changed Since The 2026-06-08 Report

The June 8 report described fitted-ringdown/source-shape stress as the current
frontier around output 740-870. Since then, the archive has grown through output
1218, and the later experiments show a clearer policy picture:

- Ringdown050 became the main later synthetic stress condition.
- The optimizer was repeatedly tested across new seed/target branches.
- Source-count ladders, Tx/Rx-offset brackets, and ringdown brackets were
  evaluated as controlled intervention families.
- A holistic notebook now summarizes these experiment families rather than
  leaving conclusions scattered across individual run folders.

The biggest change is that the project now has enough repeated evidence to say
which rescue interventions are useful and where they stop being useful.

## Intervention Evidence From Experiments 740-1218

The report detected 117 multi-run experiment series:

| Series Type | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Source-density series | 62 |
| Tx/Rx-offset series | 33 |
| Ringdown-parameter series | 22 |

Outcome summary:

| Series Type | All Accepted | Mixed With Accepted Setting | All Weak |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source-density | 2 | 47 | 13 |
| Tx/Rx offset | 7 | 16 | 10 |
| Ringdown | 12 | 5 | 5 |

Interpretation:

- Source-density changes often matter, but they are not monotonic. More sources
  can rescue a weak branch, but more sources can also fail or worsen the margin.
- Tx/Rx-offset brackets are useful, especially for acquisition-sensitive target0
  cases.
- Ringdown brackets are relatively stable when already in the right regime, but
  ringdown adjustment is not a universal rescue.

## Strongest Demonstrable Result Today

The strongest current result is not one isolated experiment. It is the repeated
synthetic variable-depth/variable-radius 3-rebar workflow:

- Known true geometry: x=[150, 250, 350] mm, z=[80, 100, 120] mm,
  radius=[5, 6, 8] mm.
- FDTD-generated synthetic reference data.
- Noisy/source-mismatched seed branches.
- Fitted ringdown stress, especially ringdown050.
- Bounded coordinate/radius optimization.
- 425/425 exact selected geometries in the 740-1218 parseable archive.
- Explicit confidence reporting that flags 159 exact-but-weak rows instead of
  overstating certainty.

This is a mature synthetic inversion-and-reporting pipeline. It is still not a
field-data validated pipeline.

## Superseded Or De-Emphasized Approaches

Still superseded or de-emphasized:

- Raw Powell radius fitting as the final answer.
- Fixed-source least-squares radius fitting as the final answer.
- W2/optimal-transport style metrics as final radius selectors.
- PEBDD bandwidth as the final radius selector.
- Free material inversion as the primary way to solve radius ambiguity.
- Older common-radius and local-profile scripts as the main workflow.

These remain useful diagnostics or historical baselines, but the coordinate
optimizer plus confidence/objective diagnostics is the current mature synthetic
multi-rebar path.
